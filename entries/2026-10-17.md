But habits are really hard to build, especially with people who are interested in too many things at the same time, a sort of shiny object syndrome and a cycle of hyperfocus and a period of inactive nothingness.
---------------\
                     \________________
But another question to be asked is: is it even that important to be consistent? Isn't the reason that we are not consistent is that life is throwing at us even bigger things (which are much more important to do on a day-to-day basis and which take precedence over the usual, the old, and the already explored) ?
Also, is the shame of not being consistent really worth the anguish, as opposed to the untethered nature of going with the flow as life goes?
In a life as short as ours:
80 years 
40 years awake
20 years free from capitalist slavery 
10 years of unrestrictive health 
120 months
1440 days, how many 21 day habits are enough?
