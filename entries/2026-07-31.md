The idea was simple: can I have a surface for writing my thoughts every day, and can it be something that becomes like a digital journal of sorts without all the distractions of all the tools that exist.

And all this exists for only 365 days, an year of writing everyday, which becomes an approximation of my thoughts over the year, thus turning into a "wiki"
